05 Good to Know

Before you go

Should I bring cash?

Yes — bring your tipping money! Show your favorite performers some love. And don't worry, we'll make change for you.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 7:00 PM and the show starts at 8:00 PM. Come early to grab a drink and settle in before the lights go down.

How do I get my tickets?

Buy right here on this page. As soon as your payment goes through you'll see your tickets, each with its own QR code, and we'll send a link to your email. Show the QR code (or a screenshot of it) at the door — every guest gets their own code, even at a table.

What's the difference between GA, VIP and a table?

General Admission gets you in with open seating. VIP puts you in premium seats close to the stage. A table reserves a spot for your whole group — each guest gets their own ticket, so friends can arrive separately.

I lost my tickets.

Open the link in your confirmation email to see them again. No email? Come to the door with your ID — we can find you on the guest list by name.

Who is behind TASTE?

TASTE is created and produced by Fawnia Mondey and presented by Fitness Temple, a Las Vegas studio dedicated to pole fitness, aerial arts and movement.
