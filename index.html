Good to Know

Before you go

Should I bring cash?

Yes — bring your tipping money! Show your favorite performers some love. And don't worry, we'll make change for you.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 7:00 PM and the show starts at 8:00 PM. Come early to grab a drink and settle in before the lights go down.

Where do I buy tickets?

Tickets are sold by Gipsy Nightclub. Pick your show above and tap General Admission, VIP or Table — you'll go straight to Gipsy's ticket page to check out.

What's the difference between GA, VIP and a table?

General Admission gets you in with open seating. VIP puts you in premium seats close to the stage. A table reserves a spot for your whole group.

I have a question about my tickets.

Tickets are handled by Gipsy Nightclub, so check your Gipsy confirmation email or contact Gipsy directly for changes, refunds or lost tickets.

Who is behind TASTE?

TASTE is created and produced by Fawnia Mondey — world-renowned pole icon and owner of Pole Fitness Studio, Las Vegas' legendary pole & aerial studio.
